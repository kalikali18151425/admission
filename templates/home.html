ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

Professional education with a purpose.

The Institute of Science, Trade & Technology (ISTT) is a professional and technical higher education institute in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Established in 1998, ISTT is directed by the Advanced Welfare Foundation. Its academic programs are offered under the affiliation of the National University and the Bangladesh Technical Education Board.
